About Me

Hello! I'm a first-year student studying computer engineering with a math minor at the University of Maryland, College Park. I am also in the College Park Scholars, Science and Global Change program, where I explore science and engineering as they relate to global changes. I love finding ways to apply and adapt my skills to solve problems in a variety of fields, but with a particular interest in aerospace and satellite systems.

Each of these projects demonstrates different applications of my growing engineering and math skills. Included are research internships, satellite projects, personal engineering and coding projects, and projects from some of my classes.

Outside of these projects, I am an Amateur Radio Operator (KC3YAK) and love to learn new math!
